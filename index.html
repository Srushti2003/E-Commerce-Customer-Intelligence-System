01 — The story in the data

Three insights worth
looking at twice.

These observations come directly from the project’s exported tables and notebook. They’re historical findings, not live business performance.

FINDING / 01
97%

Most customers bought just once.

90,557 out of 93,358 customers had one delivered order. Repeat behavior is the central retention question in this dataset.

FINDING / 02
38.3%

São Paulo leads in item revenue.

Customers in SP account for R$5.07M of the R$13.22M delivered-order item revenue in the state export.

FINDING / 03
#1

Health & beauty tops the categories.

R$1.23M in delivered-order item revenue, followed by watches & gifts and bed, bath & table.

Read responsibly

A few important definitions.

  • All figures describe a historical Brazilian e-commerce dataset, not current transactions.
  • Item-price revenue excludes freight. The R$13.22M figure should not be interpreted as total payment volume.
  • Customer counts and repeat rates use delivered orders and the unique customer identifier.
  • RFM segments and scores are method-dependent; this site illustrates the SQL rules rather than inventing unexported totals.
  • The Power BI pages shown above are screenshots; the downloadable .pbix remains the original interactive report.
